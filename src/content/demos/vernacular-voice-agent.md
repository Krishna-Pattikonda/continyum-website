name: Vernacular Voice Agent
tagline: Calls customers in Hindi, Telugu or Tamil, and logs every call with a summary.
problem: Confirming orders, recovering abandoned carts and collecting feedback all work better on a phone call in the customer's own language, but calling every customer by hand doesn't scale.
howItWorks:
  - Picks up a task, such as confirming a cash-on-delivery order, following up on an abandoned cart or asking for feedback.
  - Calls the customer and speaks in Hindi, Telugu or Tamil.
  - Handles the conversation and records the outcome.
  - Logs every call with a short written summary for the team.
