## 8. Enterprise Client Data and Confidentiality

During consulting, engineering, research, and implementation engagements, Continyum may receive access to confidential business information, operational systems, datasets, and proprietary documentation.

We recognize the commercial sensitivity of such information.

Our handling of enterprise client information is guided by the following principles:

**Purpose Limitation:** Client information should be accessed and processed only for approved project objectives or other contractually authorized purposes.

**Confidentiality:** Confidential business information is handled according to applicable agreements and confidentiality obligations.

**Controlled Access:** Access should be restricted to personnel, contractors, and authorized service providers requiring the information for legitimate project responsibilities.

**Client Ownership:** Providing information to Continyum does not, by itself, transfer ownership of the client's underlying data or proprietary materials.

**Contractual Governance:** Data access, permitted processing, retention, ownership of deliverables, and deletion arrangements may be further defined through individual service agreements, non-disclosure agreements, or data processing agreements.

Nothing in this policy overrides explicit contractual commitments made to enterprise clients.
